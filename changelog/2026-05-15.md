## Trend Analysis

1. **Distributed city-scale GS maturing**: BlitzGS achieves order-of-magnitude speedup over prior baselines through parity-based GPU sharding — signals that city-scale 3DGS is approaching practical deployment timelines
2. **4DGS + physics deep integration**: Real2Sim (4DGS + differentiable MPM) joins ParticleGS + PG-3DGS + OmniPhysGS; physics-aware AD simulation emerging as a distinct sub-field with Waymo-validated results
3. **3DGS security getting serious**: GuardMarkGS is the first framework to jointly address watermarking + edit deterrence — critical as 3DGS assets become commercially valuable
4. **Feed-forward 3DGS acceleration**: Z-Order GS (CVPR 2026 Oral) and RoSplat show that spatial ordering and robust training strategies are key to closing the quality gap with per-scene optimization
5. **In-the-wild robustness**: HarmoGS addresses gradient conflict harmonization, a fundamental issue when training 3DGS from unconstrained internet photos
6. **Language-3DGS efficiency**: SCOUP achieves 400x training speedup by decoupling language representation from 3D optimization, enabling practical open-vocabulary 3D scene understanding
7. **OIT transparency**: SparseOIT addresses the glass/transparency rendering gap in 3DGS, a long-standing limitation for indoor/automotive scenes
