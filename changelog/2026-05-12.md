## Trend Analysis

1. **Physics-informed 3DGS** gaining momentum: ParticleGS (CVPR 2026 Highlight) + LagrangianSplats extend 3DGS beyond appearance to physical simulation
2. **Volumetric video streaming** emerging as deployment frontier: CAGS (SIGGRAPH 2026) bridges 3DGS compression with adaptive streaming
3. **De-CUDA trend** accelerating: 3 new cross-platform engines (Vulkan/Metal/CPU) appeared in May alone
4. **Part-level understanding** becoming important: PaMoSplat decomposes scenes into rigid parts, enabling 4D editing
