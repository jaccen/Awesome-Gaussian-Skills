## Notable Trends
1. **3DGS enters volumetric medical imaging**: GaussianPile extends surface-only 3DGS to internal/volumetric structures via slice-aware piling + differentiable PSF, opening a new frontier beyond NeRF-based approaches
2. **Dynamic SLAM without semantic priors**: Flow4DGS-SLAM decomposes optical flow instead of relying on semantic segmentation, a cleaner approach for in-the-wild dynamic scenes
3. **Instance-level 3DGS understanding matures**: Ilov3Splat pushes beyond point/segment-level to instance-level open-vocabulary, using SAM + CLIP in a GS-native framework
4. **Learnable density control gains momentum**: LeGS is the third paper (after DeG/SIGGRAPH'26, Learn2Splat) to replace heuristic density control — confirms the trend toward RL/learned optimization
5. **Unified physical 3D generation**: PhysX-Omni spans rigid + deformable + articulated in a single framework, relevant for the articulated 3DGS research direction (ArtiSplat)
