## Notable Trends
1. **Feed-forward 3DGS scaling**: ZPressor solves token bottleneck for 100+ views; VolSplat moves to voxel-aligned prediction; PM-Loss smooths depth boundaries — three independent advances for feed-forward GS
2. **Photometric ambiguity as foundational issue**: AmbiSuR (ICML 2026) proves SH coefficients serve as intrinsic ambiguity self-indicators
3. **Billion-scale training**: TideGS demonstrates out-of-core 1B+ Gaussian training on single 24GB GPU
4. **Dual-opacity primitive design**: OP2GS decouples visual opacity from instance occupancy — may become standard for object-aware rendering
5. **Optimal Transport enters 3DGS compression**: MMGS formulates Gaussian optimization as OT-based global distribution matching
6. **Skew Gaussian has two independent lines**: 3DSGS vs SNS — different authors, different parameterization strategies
