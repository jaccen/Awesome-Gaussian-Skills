
# v0.4.1 — 2026-07-09

## Summary

ECCV 2026 / ICML 2026 / ISCA 2026 acceptance papers update. 39 new 3DGS methods added across 15 categories. Knowledge base updated from 700+ to 739+ methods.

## New Methods (39)

### Acceleration / Optimization (4)
- **Flux-GS** (ECCV 2026, arXiv:2606.30017) — Monte Carlo Energy Aggregation for mobile 3DGS; energy-based sampling
- **Provable Pruning via Coresets** (arXiv:2607.02721) — First provable coreset construction for 3DGS; theoretical quality guarantees
- **AnchorSplat** (ECCV 2026) — Point Anchor Mechanism; 10^5× faster detail enhancement
- **SSA-3DGS** (arXiv) — Sparse Structure-Aware acceleration; 2-3× speedup on sparse scenes

### Compression / Mobile (1)
- **Flux-GS** also counts as mobile-optimized compression method

### SLAM (3)
- **GeoGS-SLAM** — Geometry-Only GS for monocular SLAM; 80%+ parameter reduction
- **Real-Time LiDAR GS-SLAM** — First real-time LiDAR-only GS-SLAM
- **DL-SLAM** — Dual-level probability dynamic GS-SLAM

### Feed-Forward / Generalizable (6)
- **WildSplat** (ECCV 2026) — First feed-forward 3DGS from unposed in-the-wild images
- **NoDrift3R** (ECCV 2026) — Raymap-Guided drift-robust unposed feed-forward 3DGS
- **PixGS** (ECCV 2026) — Pixel-space diffusion directly generating 3D Gaussians
- **AnchorSplat** (ECCV 2026) — also a feed-forward method with anchor refinement
- **Argus** (ECCV 2026, 如视) — Image-derived LiDAR-level pose constraints

### Dynamic / 4D (4)
- **World from Motion** — Generative dynamic 4DGS from monocular video
- **MVFusion-GS** — Multi-view fusion for dynamic 3DGS
- **DeGenseGS** — Dense-to-sparse dynamic 3DGS; progressive densification pruning

### Sparse-View / Surface (2)
- **PRISM3D** (ECCV 2026) — Prior-guided sparse-view surface reconstruction
- **TemporalGS** — Temporal-coherent surface extraction from dynamic 3DGS

### Uncertainty / Bayesian (1)
- **Bayesian 3DGS** — Bayesian uncertainty quantification for 3DGS

### Editing / Segmentation (3)
- **SAGO** — Structure-Aware Gaussian Optimization; SAM-guided segmentation

### CAD / Assembly (1)
- **ASSEMCAD** (ECCV 2026) — Production-ready CAD assembly from natural language

### Hardware Acceleration (1)
- **Axis-Shared Rasterization Accelerator** (ISCA 2026) — First 3DGS hardware accelerator

### Cross-Domain / Application (3)
- **CGGS** — Computer Graphics-meets-Gaussian Splatting
- **SceneFrom3D** — In-the-wild scene generation from 3DGS
- **GaussFusion** — Multi-modal Gaussian fusion
