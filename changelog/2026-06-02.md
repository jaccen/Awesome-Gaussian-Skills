## Notable Trends

1. **Hierarchical rendering architecture unlocks 15.8x speedup**: HiGS (NVIDIA) discovers that 3DGS's single-tile-size paradigm forces a fundamental trade-off between partitioning efficiency (large tiles) and rasterization efficiency (small tiles). Decoupling these scales achieves order-of-magnitude rendering speedup without quality loss. This likely establishes the new baseline for all future 3DGS renderers.

2. **Ray-Gaussian intersection bridges rasterization and ray tracing**: DDF-GS demonstrates that 3DGS scenes can be distilled into neural distance fields supporting constant-time ray queries, enabling global illumination (shadows, ambient occlusion) without mesh extraction. This fundamentally changes the assumption that 3DGS can only do primary-ray rasterization.

3. **Variational/learnable density control replaces heuristics**: VEDAL (variational free energy), LeGS (learnable RL-based), and CAdam (first moment interference principle) collectively signal a paradigm shift from hand-crafted split/clone/prune thresholds to principled statistical/machine-learning approaches. 85-97% Gaussian reduction is achievable without quality loss.

4. **Knowledge-constrained CAD reconstruction**: KDH-CAD demonstrates 92.6% accuracy with only 250 labeled samples by combining pretrained foundation models with structured domain knowledge from CAD textbooks. This opens a new "knowledge-data hybrid" pathway for 3DGS→CAD that bypasses the data scarcity bottleneck.

5. **VLM-driven procedural 3D generation**: SEIG and 3DCodeBench establish VLM→code→3D as a viable alternative to neural implicit generation. The key advantage: procedural models have semantic structure, are editable, and are simulation-ready — exactly the properties that 3DGS representations lack.

6. **Multi-sensor fusion for driving NVS**: StreetNVS demonstrates that jointly conditioning on LiDAR + multi-camera + ego-motion via ray-level positional encoding matches methods requiring 10-100x denser point clouds. This makes sparse-sensor autonomous driving NVS practically feasible.
