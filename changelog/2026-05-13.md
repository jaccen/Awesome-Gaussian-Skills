## Trend Analysis

1. **Exact rendering vs. splatting approximation**: 3DGEER challenges the foundational splatting assumption — significant for fisheye/autonomous driving where approximation errors compound at wide FOV
2. **Progressive 4DGS streaming** emerging as deployment key: PD-4DGS reduces first-frame latency from hundreds of seconds to ~1.7s; DASH/HLS compatibility suggests 3DGS video streaming approaching production readiness
3. **Physics-3DGS coupling** deepens: PG-3DGS joins ParticleGS + FieryGS + LagrangianSplats — physics-guided reconstruction becoming a distinct sub-field
4. **Cross-platform maturity**: brush (Rust/WebGPU) covers 5 platforms; Godot 4 integration joins PlayCanvas — 3DGS escaping CUDA+browser binary
5. **Multi-agent SLAM**: MAGS-SLAM introduces collaborative mapping — relevant for multi-robot exploration and swarm intelligence scenarios
6. **SIGGRAPH 2026 papers concentrated**: GeoQuery + VidSplat + HairGPT + SAND + CAGS — SIGGRAPH 2026 may be the 3DGS-richest venue yet
7. **Sparse-view remains hot**: 3 new methods (GeoQuery, PairDropGS, VidSplat) in one week; diffusion priors + geometry guidance converging as dominant paradigm
