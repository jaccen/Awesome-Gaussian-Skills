## Key Trends Observed
1. **Spatially Varying Primitives** — SVGS introduces position-dependent color/opacity within individual Gaussians, a fundamental primitive representation innovation that could reduce Gaussian count for texture-rich scenes; aligns with "rendering formulation innovation" trend
2. **LLM-Driven 3D Generation** — SceneGen-LLMRL couples LLM scene understanding with RL interaction, representing the convergence of language models and 3D content creation
3. **Active Illumination Capture** — DiffAdapt4DSI brings differentiable structured illumination into GS-adjacent pipelines, expanding the capture modalities beyond passive multi-view
4. **Democratized Face Capture** — DelightingFace enables studio-quality facial appearance from smartphone video + open-sources 4K relightable dataset, lowering barrier for digital human research
5. **SIGGRAPH 2026 Cluster** — 2 of 4 new papers publish at SIGGRAPH 2026 (DelightingFace, NBV-Bayesian context), indicating continued top-venue adoption
