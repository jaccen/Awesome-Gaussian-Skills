## Method Highlights

### Speedy-Splat (CVPR 2025) — Acceleration
**Authors:** Alex Hanson, Allen Tu, Geng Lin, Vasu Singla, Matthias Zwicker, Tom Goldstein (UMD)

Identifies and addresses two key inefficiencies in 3D-GS rendering:
1. **SnugBox** — precise axis-aligned bounding box from alpha-thresholded ellipse extent (1.82× speedup, lossless)
2. **AccuTile** — exact Gaussian-tile intersection by iterating ellipse-boundary intersections (1.99× speedup, lossless)
3. **Soft Pruning** — reparameterized Hessian pruning score (36× less storage than PUP 3D-GS), 80% pruning during densification (3.14× speedup)
4. **Hard Pruning** — 30% pruning every 3000 iters post-densification (6.71× speedup, 10.6× compression, 1.47× training speedup)

Evaluated on Mip-NeRF 360, Tanks & Temples, Deep Blending. PSNR 27.55 → 26.94 (-0.61 dB) at 10.6× compression.
Project page: https://speedysplat.github.io/

### Other 8 methods
- **AESplat**: Pose-free feed-forward 3DGS, decoupled SH; +0.8 dB over NAS3R, +1.1 dB over DepthSplat on RealEstate10K
- **AGILE-GS**: SE(3) anchor-guided NBV; selection latency reduced 1-2 orders of magnitude
- **OC-GS**: Object-centric GS for irregular turntable; 12/8/6 views → 21.26/19.36/15.83 dB
- **ClearGS**: Reliability-aware view allocation + render-guided in-video restoration; SOTA on GS2E/GSOTM
- **ChronoFuseGS**: Multi-temporal Gaussian fusion with per-splat persistence; PG 2026
- **TangoGS**: Capture-derived learning allowance + training-quality guided density control; 48% fewer Gaussians
- **RRTO-CF3DGS**: Reliability-regulated trajectory optimization for COLMAP-free 3DGS; SOTA on T&T and CO3D-V2
- **Observation-Gram**: Per-Gaussian observation Gram matrix for SH compression; +0.49 dB over Compressed3D
