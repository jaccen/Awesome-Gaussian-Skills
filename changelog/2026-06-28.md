### Academic Frontiers Summary (2026 H1)

1. **Compression paradigm trifurcation**: ZipSplat (feed-forward clustering), Prune Wisely (adaptive pruning + DoG), ProGS (progressive encoding) — three distinct approaches to Gaussian efficiency
2. **Dynamic scene paradigm shift**: Structure-guided vs Gaussian-centric debate crystallizing (Beyond Static Gaussians); Liquid Neural Networks as third path with continuous-time modeling
3. **Physics-aware 3DGS**: RoVES (physics editing), RAF (physics engine abstraction), PDEO (PDE optimizer) — converging toward physically-grounded Gaussian representations
4. **Multi-sensor fusion**: LR-SGS proves LiDAR reflectance as material channel for Gaussians — key AD breakthrough
5. **Geometry-opacity decoupling**: Geometry Gaussians formally proves default 3DGS unsuited for joint texture+geometry — fundamental insight for future work
