## Notable Trends

1. **Feed-forward 3DGS dominates**: 7 of 15 new methods are feed-forward (ArtSplat, TriSplat, NoPo4D, ForeSplat, TokenGS, LangFlash, F-RNG). The trend toward single-pass generalization accelerates, with ArtSplat (400x faster) and F-RNG (25x faster) setting new efficiency benchmarks.

2. **Articulated/interactive 3DGS emerges**: ArtSplat is the first feed-forward articulated 3DGS, building on SK-GS (NeurIPS 2024) and ArtMesh (2026) but achieving 400x speedup through per-pixel joint maps. This bridges optimization-based articulated GS with real-time deployment.

3. **Degradation-aware 3DGS diversifies**: Three new environment-specific methods (Underwater360, GlowGS, DelowlightSplat) expand beyond the existing NIRRGB-GS/MERID-GS, covering underwater panoramic, nighttime glow, and extreme low-light — each with domain-specific physics models.

4. **New rendering formulations**: DP-GES introduces sort-free rendering via Depth Peeling for surfels, while TriSplat replaces Gaussian primitives entirely with oriented triangles — both challenge the standard alpha-compositing pipeline.

5. **4DGS security**: 4D-GSW is the first watermarking method designed specifically for 4DGS, extending GuardMarkGS (static) into the temporal domain with kinematic-aware STC metrics.

6. **Feed-forward compositional avatars**: COSY and SplitAvatar independently tackle component-level generation/editing of head avatars, enabling granular control without segmentation masks.
