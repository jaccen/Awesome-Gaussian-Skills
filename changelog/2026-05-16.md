## Trend Analysis

1. **Non-Gaussian primitives gaining traction**: SNS (Skew-Normal) joins Softmax-GS and 3DGEER in challenging the standard Gaussian kernel — the community is systematically exploring more expressive primitive distributions beyond symmetric ellipsoids
2. **Expert routing for demand-driven allocation**: SplatWeaver's cardinality expert routing (0/1/2/3 Gaussians per pixel) achieves dramatic efficiency gains (30% budget, +1.02 dB) — signals a shift from uniform to adaptive allocation in feed-forward 3DGS
3. **Continuous LoD without architectural modification**: MGS (Matryoshka GS) enables training-time LoD via stochastic budget sampling — simpler than explicit LoD hierarchies (Scaffold-GS, Octree-GS) and composable with any backbone
4. **3DGS optimization reframed as denoising**: Denoising-GS treats primitive optimization as a spatial denoising problem — connects 3DGS training to the well-established denoising literature
5. **Feedforward 3DGS repair for driving**: ConFixGS addresses the underexplored problem of fixing feedforward 3DGS outputs with diffusion priors — critical for deploying feedforward models in safety-critical applications
