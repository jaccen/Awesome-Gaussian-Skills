## Key Trends Observed
1. **Light Field Displays** — CoherentRaster extends 3DGS beyond 2D screens to LFD rendering (new application domain)
2. **Audio-Visual 3DGS** — AudioGS extends Gaussian Splatting from visual to audio modality (cross-modal expansion)
3. **Multi-spectral Imaging** — MSGS brings 3DGS to remote sensing / material identification (domain expansion)
4. **Security & Robustness** — PatchPoison reveals adversarial vulnerabilities in 3DGS pipelines (emerging concern)
5. **Off-road AD Reconstruction** — Ground4D addresses structured-road limitation, expanding 3DGS AD to unstructured environments
6. **CVPR 2026 highlights** — ULF-Loc (Highlight), Ilov3Splat (ICPR 2026), QuadBox (ICIP 2026) signal growing top-venue adoption
