## Trend Analysis

1. **3DGS + Embodied AI is a rapidly growing intersection**: 7 papers across T-RO, CoRL, ECCV, RSS, RAL — spanning grasping, manipulation, navigation, and reasoning — demonstrate that 3DGS's explicit, editable representation is becoming the preferred 3D backbone for embodied perception over NeRF
2. **GraspSplats' NeRF critique is significant**: The method proves NeRF is fundamentally unsuitable for scene changes (required in manipulation) due to implicit nature — a strong argument for 3DGS in interactive robotics
3. **Sim-to-Real via 3DGS is establishing standard practice**: VR-Robo (RAL 2025) and RoboSplat (RSS 2025) independently converge on 3DGS-based R2S2R pipelines for robot learning
4. **3DGS as spatial memory (GSMem)** bridges representation and reasoning — treating Gaussian scenes as persistent, queryable memory for zero-shot embodied QA
5. **Data augmentation via Gaussian manipulation (RoboSplat)** directly operates on Gaussian primitives rather than images or meshes — a uniquely 3DGS-native approach to scaling robot demonstration data
