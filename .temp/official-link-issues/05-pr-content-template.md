## Tips for Successful Submissions

1. **Always check CONTRIBUTING.md first** — follow the repo's exact process
2. **Match the existing format exactly** — entry format, indentation, spacing
3. **Keep descriptions concise** — 1 line, no more than 15 words
4. **Demonstrate relevance** — explain why the project fits the list's scope
5. **Be patient** — maintainers may take weeks to respond
6. **Don't self-promote aggressively** — frame as "this helps the community" not "look at my project"
7. **One PR per project** — don't bundle multiple additions in one PR (unless the list explicitly allows it)
8. **Respond promptly to maintainer feedback** — be ready to adjust the entry format, category, or description
