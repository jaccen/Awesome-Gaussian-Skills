## Method Highlights

- **MOMASPLAT** — Dynamic novel-view demonstration augmentation for mobile manipulation, improving
  visuomotor-policy camera-viewpoint robustness.
- **InstantHand** — Feed-forward single-image animatable hand avatar reconstruction via MANO.
- **Spaghetti Splatting** — Differentiating curvilinear Dirac masses via convolution for curve/line
  primitive splatting.
- **FastWild-GS** — Minutes-scale in-the-wild appearance adaptation with compact per-image residuals.
- **RetinexGS** — Retinex-inspired low-light RAW reconstruction.
- **DepthGS** — Depth-guided adaptive regularization for high-quality surface reconstruction.
- **GEMOR-GS** — Global extrinsic-calibration-refined mapping + online relocalization for
  LiDAR-inertial-visual Gaussian-splatting SLAM.
- **CP-GS** — Capacity-precision coupled Gaussian splatting for budget-aware 2D Gaussian image representation.
