## Notable Trends

1. **Structured 3DGS acceleration converges on occlusion-aware rendering**: Proxy-GS (proxy mesh culling) and HiGS (hierarchical tiles) both attack the same bottleneck — decoding invisible Gaussians. Proxy-GS' full-score oral at CVPR 2026 validates the occlusion-prior approach.

2. **Extreme pruning reaches 90% with DoG primitives**: Prune Wisely demonstrates that Difference-of-Gaussian primitives can replace standard Gaussians for efficiency, suggesting a new primitive type alongside 3D/2D/surfel/triangle/token.

3. **CAD generation enters agentic era**: Zero-to-CAD (Autodesk) shows a 2B VLM trained purely on synthetic CAD programs outperforms GPT-5.2, confirming the data-centric approach over model scaling for domain-specific tasks.

4. **B-rep understanding gains multimodal CLIP**: BRepCLIP bridges the gap between geometric B-rep representations and language understanding, enabling text-to-CAD retrieval and QA without explicit parameterization.

5. **Satellite 3D reconstruction emerges as application**: SatSurfGS opens orbital-scale 3DGS reconstruction, a new vertical beyond street-level (driving) and aerial (city-scale).

6. **3D vision comprehensive surveys mature**: Two major surveys (Cookbook of 3D Vision, 3D Representation Survey) establish a canonical taxonomy, reducing fragmentation in the field.
